Figure 2 | Benchmark comparison on the same 100 targets. a, inventory and strict closure counts; labels show strict / inventory. b, first-route mean ChemCensor (CC) across all 100 targets, with missing or invalid routes scored zero. c, paired strict-closure outcomes for the same targets. d, inventory-closed route diversity averaged across targets, total inventory-closed output routes with complete yield evidence, and the conditional mean route yield proxy among completely covered routes. RetroAgent is the final 100-target prequential-memory run, with 99 inventory closures, 82 strict closures and mean first-route CC 2.7349. DeepRetro uses the original author repository (dc8fcad), with a local GPT-6.1 Sol Medium execution transport and the common structure, inventory and pinned ChemCensor audit. Of 100 DeepRetro targets, 97 executions completed and 3 had execution errors; the 100-target denominator is retained. The other five local adapted agent implementations are excluded. Four specialized-model methods excluded under the archived whole-method execution-error rule remain excluded, leaving 14 valid methods. Dashes denote metrics not available from the original DeepRetro export and are not treated as zero. Yield evidence is the archived relaxed-rule evaluation and is not an experimental yield measurement.

Figure 3 | Measured effects of within-target continuation in the final 100-target RetroAgent run. a, inventory and strict closure at the phase-one checkpoint and at the end of the same run. b, paired first-route CC; rose points denote targets rescued to strict closure and other targets are blue. The dashed line is equality. c, counts of final outcomes conditional on checkpoint status. d, individual CC changes grouped by retained strict closure, rescued strict closure and unresolved targets; horizontal marks show group means. e, final / checkpoint compute ratio; annotations are checkpoint / final means per target for model calls, LLM calls, search work and elapsed seconds. f, rescue fractions among initially open or stock-only targets, followed by the fraction of strict rescues with a changed target disconnection. Continuation retains all 72 initially strictly closed targets, rescues 10 more, and improves average CC from 2.45 to 2.73. Nine of ten strict rescues change the first disconnection. These panels compare the checkpoint and final outputs of the same run. Elapsed values are mean case execution times under the archived concurrent execution, not total batch wall time.
